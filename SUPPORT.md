# Getting help

Locus is maintained by one person in spare time, so please look here first.

## Read first

- [README.md](README.md), especially the [FAQ](README.md#faq).
- [docs/STATE.md](docs/STATE.md): what's built, and what hasn't been tried on a phone yet.
- [docs/design/](docs/design/): one note per feature, with how it behaves and why.
- [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md): what changed in each version.

## Something broken?

Open a [bug report](https://github.com/springmusk026/locus/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with your Locus version, device, Android version and model provider. Settings → About & privacy → Diagnostics can share a local log with keys and passwords removed; read it before you paste it.

## An idea?

Open a [feature request](https://github.com/springmusk026/locus/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml). Check [docs/ROADMAP.md](docs/ROADMAP.md) first.

## A security problem?

Don't open an issue. Use [private reporting](https://github.com/springmusk026/locus/security/advisories/new), as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes.

## Not covered here

Questions about a provider's billing, quotas or model behaviour go to that provider. Locus can't see or change your account with them.
