# Heat pumps in cold climates

2026-11-21

## Summary

Modern cold-climate air-source heat pumps keep most of their heating capacity down to about -15 °C [1], and
field trials in Nordic homes report seasonal efficiency well above resistance heating [2, 3]. Claims of full
capacity at -30 °C come from one manufacturer and couldn't be confirmed.

## How do they perform below freezing?

Rated capacity at -15 °C is typically 70–100% of nominal for units sold as "cold climate" [1]. A Norwegian
field study measured an average seasonal COP of 2.8 across 40 homes [2].

## What do they cost to run?

Running costs depend on the electricity price; one utility's comparison found them cheaper than oil
heating in every month of the trial [3].

## Unverified claims

- One manufacturer says its unit keeps full capacity at -30 °C. Only the maker's own page says so, and no test confirms it.
