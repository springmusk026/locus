### Changed
- **The JVM tests no longer sleep or race the clock.** Every `Thread.sleep` in `src/test` is gone: tests now wait for the event itself (a process's exit, a service's exit watcher, the background deletion of a chat's browser pages, the publish sheet's next step) instead of a guessed time, and tests that sorted rows made milliseconds apart stamp their times instead of sleeping between them. Assertions that compared with the clock after the fact (remote mode's end time, a research report's dated path) now compare with a time read before the action. Two small hooks make this possible without changing behaviour: a Linux service can say when its exit has been noted, and the browser page store can wait for deletions it started. JVM tests for the app module are still to come. (#332)
