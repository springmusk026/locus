### Fixed
- **Outside text can no longer hide data in variation selectors or split the closing tag with one.** Locus kept one selector after any visible character, so a page could hide a byte after every letter, and `</untrusted` + U+034F (or a Mongolian selector, U+FE00–FE0F, U+2800) + `_web_content>` got past the defusing. A selector now stays only where it applies (emoji style after a symbol, variants after a CJK ideograph, Mongolian selectors after Mongolian letters, a combining grapheme joiner between two marks), the closing tag is found past anything that draws nothing of its own, cleaning twice gives the same text as cleaning once, and NUL, other control characters and terminal escape sequences are removed from marked tool results. (#393)
