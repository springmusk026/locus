### Fixed
- **Allow and Continue are no longer offered on notifications below Android 12.** Android 8 to 11 can't make a notification button ask for the unlock first, so with Lock Locus off anyone holding a locked phone could allow a waiting action, or let a paused task spend more, from the lock screen. There, an approval now offers only Deny and a budget pause only Stop; tap the notification to open Locus and allow or continue. Android 12 and later are unchanged. See [app lock](docs/design/app-lock.md#notifications). (#367)
- **Other apps can no longer move the whole workspace or write outside it through the Files app.** An app you gave a workspace folder to could rename the workspace itself out of reach, create a file outside the workspace by picking a name that was already taken, or reach a sibling folder through `..` in a folder grant. The workspace can't be renamed or deleted now, names are single names (no `.` or `..`) that stay in their folder, a taken name becomes `a (1).txt` instead of `a.txt (1)`, a grant covers only what is really inside its folder, and a file another app overwrites keeps its earlier version in Workspace → Trash. See [android bridge](docs/design/android-bridge.md#the-workspace-in-other-apps). (#368)
- **Another app can't pick an existing chat for something it shares.** A share naming a chat (Direct Share) opened that chat with the shared text whatever the chat, and any app can name one. Now it lands there only when the chat is one the share sheet offers right now (pinned and recent chats; never a locked, hidden or older one, and none with Lock Locus on); otherwise it goes to a new chat, as a plain share does. Nothing was ever sent without your Send. See [android bridge](docs/design/android-bridge.md#ways-in). (#374)
