### Fixed
- **Dates, times, money and chat exports follow your language and clock.** Reminders showed English day and month names on a 24-hour clock, Insights, the chat list and ChatGPT's reset time used fixed patterns (so Chinese read "11月 10" instead of "11月10日"), and costs were written the US way ("≈$1.24") in Insights, receipts, budgets, Compare and the budget editor.
