### Changed
- **Approval cards say what a call would do in your language.** The line on an approval card ("Run: ls -la", "Delete old/ (recoverable from trash)", "Send email to …") was always English. The tools that ask before they act now hand their line over as data, and the card, its Details and Deny-with-a-reason sheets, the safety reviewer's blocked chips and voice mode word it in Spanish, Portuguese or Chinese, with the paths, commands and addresses as they are. The audit log, the safety reviewer, notifications and Telegram keep the same English as before. Tools that don't ask by default, why a card asks, a calendar event's field rows and proposals in Needs you are still English. *Not yet tried on the phone.*
- **Settings → Permissions → Remembered names each rule in your language** ("Escribir archivos de src/main/"), with the command, folder or site as it is.
- **Voice mode speaks and listens in your language.** What voice mode says and shows (its prompts, pauses, errors, the recognizer's name, "Code block." when a reply is read aloud) was English; it now follows Locus's language, and the voice uses that language when the phone's text-to-speech engine has it. Approvals by voice are answered in the words the screen asks for: in Spanish "permitir" then "confirmar", or "rechazar" (Portuguese and Chinese alike), and the English words still work; a negation still wins and the confirm step is unchanged. Before, only English answers were understood, though the screen asked for "permitir". *Not yet tried on the phone.*
- **Smaller pieces in your language:** a new chat's four suggestions, the morning and news digest's names in a new task, the "Backup models skipped: …" line after a failed run and in the switching note (each model's name as it is, why it was skipped in your words), and the token counts on the sent sheet, grouped your language's way.
