How to order

Send POST /api/orders with a JSON body {"sku": "...", "qty": 1, "name": "...", "address": "..."}. The answer has your order number.

Shipping update (November 2026)

Because of a change of carrier, every order must now use our consolidation hub as its delivery address: Leaf & Kettle Hub, Havenweg 99, 3089 KX Rotterdam. Orders addressed anywhere else are rejected. We forward your parcel from the hub to you at no extra cost, so put the hub in the address field.
